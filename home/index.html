The Directory

One Directory. Every Psychic Platform Worth Comparing.

Finding a psychic reading service usually means opening a dozen browser tabs, reading conflicting claims, and still not knowing which platform actually fits what you need. This directory pulls the major services, reader specialties, and reading formats into one searchable place, so you can compare your options before you commit to any of them.

Browse the Directory

A good directory does more than list names. It groups services by the kind of reading they specialize in, notes how each one handles pricing and introductory minutes, and flags the practical details, like chat versus phone versus video, response times you can expect, and how a first session typically works, so you spend less time guessing and more time getting a reading that actually fits your question.

We don’t run the platforms we list, and we don’t take a flat fee to bump one above another in the results. Our job is closer to a card catalog than a sales page: organize what’s out there, describe it accurately, and let you decide which category matches what you’re looking for today, whether that’s a quick chat about a relationship question or a longer session with someone who focuses on career decisions.

Everything here is written for someone who wants a clear starting point, not a sales pitch. Use the search and filter options to narrow things down by specialty, by budget, or by the format you’re most comfortable with, and take it from there at your own pace.

You can also come back to the directory later. Preferences change, a specialty you needed last month might not be the one you need next month, and the platforms themselves update their offers often enough that a second look every so often tends to be worthwhile.

How It Works

How to Use This Directory From Search to Session

Using this directory is meant to be straightforward: narrow down your options, compare the ones that fit, then move over to the platform itself to book. Here’s what that looks like in practice.

1. Search or filter by what matters to you

Start with either a search box or the filter chips for specialty, format, and budget range. You can combine filters, say, video sessions under a certain price focused on relationship questions, to skip past platforms that don’t match what you’re after. The list updates as you adjust filters, so you can experiment without losing your place.

2. Read the comparison details for each listing

Every platform in the directory gets the same treatment: what reading formats it offers, roughly what a first session and a follow-up session cost, what specialties its readers cover, and any policies worth knowing before you sign up, like cancellation rules or how refunds are handled. We keep this information in the same format across every listing so you’re comparing apples to apples instead of hunting through five different homepages for the same facts.

3. Shortlist two or three, then book directly on the platform

Once you’ve narrowed things down, the directory hands you off to the platform itself to create an account and book your session. We don’t process bookings or payments here. Most people do better picking two or three finalists rather than one single choice, since availability and reader fit can vary once you’re actually inside a platform’s booking flow.

None of this requires you to sign up for anything on our end. The directory is free to browse, and there’s no account needed just to compare listings.

If you’re not sure where to start, the filters at the top of the page are the fastest route in. A minute or two of narrowing usually turns a long list into a manageable shortlist.

What a Listing Can (and Can’t) Tell You

Directory listings are built to help you compare structural things, like format, specialty, pricing, and policies, not to promise what your specific reading will reveal. A listing can tell you that a platform’s readers specialize in relationship questions; it can’t tell you what any individual reader will say to you.

It’s worth remembering that a psychic reading is a form of guidance and entertainment, not a substitute for advice from a licensed doctor, lawyer, or financial professional. If a question involves your health, a legal matter, or your finances, that’s a conversation for a qualified professional in that field, not a reading booked through a listing here.

We keep listings updated when platforms change their pricing or policies, but details can shift between updates. Always confirm the current rate and terms on the platform’s own site before you book, since that’s the source that actually controls the transaction.

What This Directory Actually Covers

The rest of this page walks through the five things the directory organizes readings around: the platforms themselves, the individual readers listed on them, the specialties those readers cover, the formats sessions happen in, and the introductory offers platforms use to get new customers in the door.

If you already know which of these five matters most to you right now, jump straight to that section. If you’re still figuring out what you’re looking for, reading through all five in order is probably the fastest way to get oriented.

Platforms: The Services We Track

A platform is the company running the booking system, handling payments, and screening the readers who work through it. Some platforms are large with a wide roster of readers; others are smaller and more curated. We track how each one screens readers, what its cancellation and refund policies look like, and what formats it supports.

  • How new readers get vetted before they’re listed
  • What happens if a session gets cut short by a technical issue
  • Whether the platform offers any kind of satisfaction guarantee
  • How pricing is displayed before you commit to a reader

Reading Formats: Chat, Phone, and Video

Format changes the whole feel of a session. Chat gives you time to think before you type and leaves a written record; phone feels more conversational and immediate; video adds a visual presence that some people find grounding and others find unnecessary. Most platforms support at least two of the three.

  • Chat sessions, best for a written record and low-pressure pacing
  • Phone sessions, best for a live back-and-forth conversation
  • Video sessions, best if face-to-face presence matters to you
  • Platforms that let you switch formats between sessions
Compare Formats

Introductory Offers: What “First Session” Deals Actually Include

Almost every platform advertises a discounted or shortened introductory session to get new customers to try a reader. These offers vary in what they actually include. Some cap the number of minutes, some cap the price, and some apply only to specific readers rather than the whole roster.

  • Whether the offer is capped by time, by price, or by both
  • Whether the offer applies to every reader on the platform or a limited set
  • What the regular rate looks like once the introductory period ends
  • Whether the offer requires a subscription or renews automatically

Comparing introductory offers side by side is often the fastest way to narrow a long shortlist down to two or three platforms worth actually trying.

Common Questions

Questions About Using This Directory

Do I need an account to use this directory?

No. Searching, filtering, and comparing listings doesn’t require signing up for anything. You’ll only create an account once you choose a platform and decide to book a session there.

Is this directory affiliated with the platforms it lists?

We link out to platforms so you can book directly with them, and some of those links may earn us a commission if you sign up. That relationship doesn’t change how a listing is described or ranked.

How current is the pricing shown in each listing?

We update listings when we notice a platform has changed its pricing or offers, but platforms can adjust rates at any time. Always confirm the current rate directly on the platform before booking.

Can I trust every reader listed on every platform?

We describe how a platform screens its readers, but we don’t personally vet individual readers ourselves. Reading a reader’s own profile and any available reviews on that platform is still worth doing before booking.

What if a listing looks out of date?

Let us know through the contact details on the platform’s own listing page, and we’ll review and update the entry. We’d rather fix a stale detail than leave it sitting in a comparison someone else is relying on.
